# Project Constraints Essay

**Project:** ReFrame  
**Team Members:** Maria Malik, Aradana Nair, Priyanshi Kulshreshtha, Mbathio Lo, Hinna Parwez 

## Professional

- The tool will evaluate student reflections tied to their co-op experience, so there is a professional responsibility to ensure that the assessment is applied consistently across students.
- The AI should use the same criteria for all students and avoid unfair or biased evaluations.
- Sentiment analysis will help advisers recognize whether a student’s experience was positive or negative, but it will have no impact on grading.
- Students will be told clearly how their reflections will be used and scored.

## Ethical

- Student evaluations can sometimes be personal or vulnerable because students are encouraged to be honest about their co-op struggles and work environments. 
- Scoring that content without care could discourage students from reflecting honestly in the future. 
- Instead of allowing an AI-generated score to stand on its own, an adviser should review the evaluation before it affects a student’s standing. 
- This added human oversight can help reduce the risk of unfair or harmful assessments.
- The system should perform consistently across different writing styles and levels of English proficiency. 
- Since co-op students come from diverse backgrounds, it would be unfair for the model to give lower scores simply because a student is a non-native English speaker.

## Security

- The system will collect and analyze text reflection from about 25 students. 
- It will protect the data at a level appropriate for student educational records. 
- Access to individual reflections, data from the Power BI dashboard and any sentiment analysis output should be limited to the advisor responsible for that student. 
- Storage location is not finalized yet, it will either be through a UC-managed system or a third-party cloud system.
- Student reflections would be kept confidential until they opt in for having their information used for research or similar purposes.
